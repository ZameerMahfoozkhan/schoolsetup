Weatherproof Open Gym Stations Across Gaya & Bodh Gaya

Gaya is actively expanding community fitness facilities across landmark open spaces like Gandhi Maidan Gaya, Kendui Park, AP Colony, Civil Lines, and Rampur, along with extensive university sports complexes at Central University of South Bihar (Panchanpur) and spiritual retreat parks in Bodh Gaya.

South Bihar's climate presents intense summer thermal extremes (often >44°C) and arid dusty winds that cause standard low-grade gym equipment to seize and rust. School Setup engineers open gym systems with heavy Class-B galvanized iron (GI) piping, heat-resistant powder coatings cured at 200°C, and fully sealed self-lubricating Japanese bearings that ensure zero-maintenance operation without electrical dependency.
