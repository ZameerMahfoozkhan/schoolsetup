Turnkey Methodology

5-Step Campus Handover Process

STEP 1

Requirement & Space Audit

We review your architectural plans, classroom dimensions, student enrollment projections, and playground open areas.

STEP 2

2D Layout & Itemized BOQ

Our designers produce 2D layout drawings showing exact furniture placements and deliver transparent institutional pricing.

STEP 3

Precision Manufacturing

Frames, desks, and play units are fabricated with robotic welding, pure powder coating, and strict quality audits.

STEP 4

Consolidated Road Dispatch

Dedicated transport moves your complete order straight to your Ayodhya campus with zero courier transshipment damage.

STEP 5

On-Site Handover

Our fitting technicians unpack, place, and grout equipment into position, delivering ready-to-use learning spaces.
