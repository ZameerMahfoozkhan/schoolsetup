Heavy Commercial Play Systems

Engineered for Gaya's Intense Sun & Dynamic Campus Play

Schools and institutions across AP Colony, Civil Lines, Rampur, Kendui, Tekari Road, and Bodh Gaya are investing heavily in world-class sports and recreation infrastructure. However, South Bihar's extreme arid summer heat—frequently surpassing 44°C to 46°C—wrecks havoc on conventional playground equipment, causing cheap thin plastics to warp, bleach, and develop brittle cracks within months.

School Setup engineers playground systems built specifically to master these climatic stresses. Featuring 114mm OD heavy galvanized steel uprights, rotomolded virgin LLDPE with UV-Grade 8 stabilizers, and thermoset polyester powder coating baked at 200°C, our play equipment stays structurally rock-solid and visually brilliant year after year.

Frequently Asked Questions (Gaya Playground Equipment)

Practical engineering and installation insights for school management, trust boards, and municipal buyers in Gaya.

Gaya endures some of the most scorching summers in North India, with direct solar exposure regularly pushing surface temperatures above 50°C. Standard commercial plastics degrade quickly under these conditions. School Setup manufactures all playground slides, roofs, and crawl tubes using food-grade virgin LLDPE formulated with UV-Grade 8 stabilizers and Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers (HALS). This prevents photo-oxidation, surface chalking, thermal micro-cracking, and color bleaching for over a decade of outdoor exposure.

Our skilled installation team carries heavy mechanical pit augers and breakers. We excavate 600mm to 750mm deep foundation pits and pour structural M20 reinforced cement concrete (RCC) footings around each 114mm GI upright, ensuring absolute dynamic stability even during vigorous recess play.
