Turnkey Methodology

5-Step Campus Handover Process for Kanpur

Step 01

Campus Architecture Review

We analyze your architectural CAD files, room count targets, and age demographic distribution across Kanpur campuses.

Step 02

BOQ & Spatial Planning

We build an itemized Bill of Quantities alongside 2D seating layouts conforming to CBSE/ICSE circulation and evacuation codes.

Step 03

Robotic Batch Manufacturing

Automated laser tube cutting, robotic welding, 7-tank anti-corrosion pre-treatment, and pure polyester electrostatic powder coating.

Step 04

Consolidated Highway Dispatch

Dispatched in dedicated covered freight trucks via NH-27 directly to your Kanpur campus gate, eliminating transshipment damage.

Step 05

Civil Grouting & Room Placement

Technician crews unbox, level, and place classroom furniture, and cast M20 concrete footings for playground stations.

Step 06

Handover & Warranty Certificate

Institutional sign-off, safety compliance certificate handover, and priority warranty support for future academic wings.
