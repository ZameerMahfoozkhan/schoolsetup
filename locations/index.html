Institutional Supply Logistics

How We Support Campus Orders Across Uttar Pradesh

Unlike retail traders who rely on third-party parcel services, School Setup operates institutional truck dispatches. Every delivery is packed in heavy-duty bubble wrap and corrugated protection, with on-site deployment teams ready to anchor and assemble equipment upon arrival.

1

Direct Factory-to-Campus Vehicle Dispatches

Dedicated transport avoids multi-point transit handling, protecting dual desks, swings, and outdoor gyms from scratches or structural impacts.

2

On-Site Mechanical Grouting & Assembly

Playground equipment and outdoor gyms require deep RCC civil grouting for child and public safety. Our experienced installation crew completes the job on site.

3

Serving Bulk Enquiries from Neighboring Districts

For full-school setups, multi-classroom furniture orders, or complete public park gym projects, we also provide direct dispatch to Barabanki, Amethi, Rae Bareli, Gonda, Basti, Ambedkar Nagar, Jaunpur, Pratapgarh, Unnao, and Kanpur.
