Turnkey Methodology

5-Step Lucknow Campus Handover Process

STEP 1

Blueprint & Space Audit

We review your architectural blueprints, room dimensions, target student enrollment, and outdoor ground dimensions across Lucknow.

STEP 2

2D Layout & Itemized BOQ

Our institutional planners prepare 2D layout drawings showing optimal desk alignments and clear itemized institutional rate quotations.

STEP 3

Precision Industrial Production

Steel frames, wooden tops, and playground assemblies undergo robotic MIG welding, seven-tank anti-rust coating, and dual-layer powder coating.

STEP 4

Consolidated Road Dispatch

Dedicated freight trucks transit all classroom, playground, and laboratory components straight to your Lucknow campus gate together.

STEP 5

On-Site Handover & Grouting

Our technicians position desks, anchor outdoor gym and play equipment into concrete, conduct quality checks, and hand over keys.
