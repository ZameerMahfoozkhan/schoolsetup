Company Profile

About School Setup

School Setup is an institutional solutions provider focused on school furniture, kids preschool furniture, commercial playground equipment, and public outdoor gyms. We partner directly with educational trusts, school owners, architects, and community developers across Uttar Pradesh.

Built for Institutional Buyers

Our Core Business Focus

We bridge the gap between heavy industrial manufacturing and institutional educational procurement.

Material Standards

We use heavy-gauge CRCA steel pipes, multi-stage chemical anti-rust pretreatments, pure polyester powder-coating, and virgin LLDPE rotational plastics to ensure extreme physical longevity.

Ergonomics & Safety

Every student desk and chair is designed around natural posture standards to reduce fatigue during extended classroom study hours. All outdoor play systems prioritize fall safety and rounded edge geometry.

Direct Logistics & Civil Setup

We don't merely drop boxes at your campus gate. Our regional teams handle direct transport dispatch, on-site classroom leveling, ground bolt foundation grouting, and final cleanup.

Company Information

Operational Standards & Compliance

Transparent institutional operations designed for school procurement committees and trust audits.

GST & Invoicing

Complete formal GST tax invoicing compliant with institutional trust accounting, audit documentation, and school finance teams.

Sample Swatches & Verification

Before batch dispatch on large orders, physical color chips, steel gauge verification, and finish samples can be evaluated by school management.

Batch Production Timelines

Clear production milestones shared for summer break classroom renovations and opening-day deadlines.

Regional Service Backing

Quick response support for spare nuts, bolts, replacement slide hoods, or extra student desk additions as student enrollment expands.
