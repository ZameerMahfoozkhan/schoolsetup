Commercial Playfields

Safe, Weather-Resistant Playgrounds for Ayodhya Institutions

As educational institutions and community parks develop across Ayodhya and Faizabad, creating safe, engaging outdoor play zones is a primary priority. School Setup manufactures outdoor play equipment using virgin UV-stabilized LLDPE plastics and hot-dip galvanized steel pipes (114mm OD main posts) with pure polyester powder coating.

Every multiplay system is delivered with full civil installation support: our technical crew excavates required foundation pits and completes concrete RCC grouting on your site to guarantee long-term stability and child safety.
