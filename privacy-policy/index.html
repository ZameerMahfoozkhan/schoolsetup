Privacy Policy

At School Setup, we value transparency and are committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of educational institutions, trustees, school owners, and procurement coordinators who contact us.

1. Information We Collect

When you request an institutional quotation, submit an enquiry form, message our WhatsApp desk, or call our customer service numbers, we may collect the following details:

  • Full Name and Professional Title (e.g., Principal, Administrator, Trustee)
  • Institution Name and Type (School, Preschool, College, Trust, Park Committee)
  • Phone Number and WhatsApp Contact Information
  • Official Email Address
  • Delivery Location (City, District, State)
  • Project Specifications, Quantity Requirements, and Timelines

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used for legitimate B2B business operations:

  • Preparing and sending institutional price quotes, PDF product catalogs, and layout diagrams.
  • Coordinating delivery schedules, transport logistics, and on-site assembly crews.
  • Responding to customer service, warranty inquiries, and post-installation support.
  • Complying with statutory tax, invoicing (GST), and regulatory requirements.

3. Data Protection & Zero Third-Party Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third-party telemarketers or advertisers. Form submissions via our website are processed securely using modern SSL/TLS encryption. Contact details are retained solely for client relationship management and legitimate institutional order processing.

4. Cookies and Analytical Metrics

Our website uses minimal technical cookies and privacy-friendly web analytics to understand traffic volume, page load speeds, and user navigation patterns. You may adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time without impacting site readability.
