Engineered Outdoor Play

Commercial Playparks Built for North Bihar's Alluvial Conditions

Schools and residential societies across Muzaffarpur's growing corridors — along Bhagwanpur, Bairiya, Motihari Road, Gobarsahi, and Mithanpura — face distinct ground conditions. The Burhi Gandak flood plain creates soft alluvial silt with high water retention during monsoons, requiring commercial playground structures engineered with specialized civil anchoring.

School Setup delivers commercial multiplay stations built around 114mm OD heavy galvanized iron (GI) steel structural pipes, anti-slip perforated safety decks, and rotational molded virgin LLDPE polymer slides. Accompanied by our dedicated on-site civil team, we excavate deep 900mm (3-foot) pits and cast reinforced M20 RCC footings with elevated plinth collars, ensuring play equipment remains perfectly rigid and corrosion-free for 10+ years.
