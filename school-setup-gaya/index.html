Turnkey Campus Infrastructure for Gaya & Bodh Gaya Institutions

Gaya is South Bihar's major cultural, spiritual, and educational hub. Driven by international monastic institutions along the Bodh Gaya and Dobhi corridors, premier schools in AP Colony, Civil Lines, and Delha, and massive university developments around the Central University of South Bihar (CUSB) at Panchanpur, school founders are investing in world-class campus facilities.

School Setup delivers a comprehensive single-source manufacturer turnkey model tailored for South Bihar. We design 2D architectural classroom seating layouts, supply heavy 16-gauge CRCA student desks, build CBSE-compliant composite science laboratories, outfit library reading stacks, and erect UV-stabilized outdoor playgrounds engineered to withstand Gaya's intense summer sun (>44°C).
