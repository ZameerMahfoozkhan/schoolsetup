School Furniture in Gaya & Bodh Gaya

Heavy-duty 16-gauge CRCA dual student desks, coaching benches, and teacher podiums engineered for schools and academies across Gaya, Bodh Gaya & Tekari.

Thermal-Resilient Desking

Engineered for Intensive Multi-Shift Academics in Gaya

Gaya's student community operates in intensive academic hubs across AP Colony, Civil Lines, Delha, and Station Road, alongside prestigious residential and international monastic schools in Bodh Gaya and Dobhi. Heavy daily student rotation combined with Gaya's dry, blistering summer heat demands furniture engineered with exceptional structural integrity.

School Setup manufactures institutional dual desks fabricated from certified 16-gauge (1.5mm wall thickness) cold-rolled close annealed (CRCA) steel with automated robotic MIG welding. Treated with heat-cured pure polyester powder coating and moisture-tight 2mm PVC edge banding, our desks withstand intensive multi-shift usage and summer temperatures exceeding 44°C without loosening, warping, or fading.
