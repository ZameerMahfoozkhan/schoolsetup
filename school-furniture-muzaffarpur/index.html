School Furniture in Muzaffarpur

Heavy-duty 16-gauge CRCA classroom dual desks, coaching benches, and teacher podiums engineered for schools and institutes across Muzaffarpur & North Bihar.

Industrial Grade Desking

Engineered for Heavy Student Traffic Across Muzaffarpur

Muzaffarpur is North Bihar's focal center for educational excellence, hosting prestigious institutions along Mithanpura and Club Road, alongside hundreds of competitive test preparation institutes around Kalambagh Road and Gannipur. Heavy multi-shift student traffic demands furniture far more durable than lightweight commercial retail stock.

School Setup manufactures institutional dual desks fabricated from certified 16-gauge (1.5mm wall thickness) cold-rolled close annealed (CRCA) steel. Protected with a 7-tank anti-rust pre-treatment and pure polyester powder coating, our desks resist North Bihar's high monsoon humidity, moisture swelling, and aggressive daily usage without squeaking, loosening, or chipping.
